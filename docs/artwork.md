## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one clean standalone landscape illustration for the right side of a premium dark software landing page. Generate exactly one original image, 1536 × 1024 pixels, 3:2 landscape.
Primary request: A monumental sharp alpine peak towers over a dark, still lake beneath vast dense storm clouds. A powerful swirling supercell curves across the upper sky and narrows into an unmistakable graceful tornado formation beside and partly behind the peak, subtly suggesting a cyclone without being a logo. Small silhouetted pine-covered ridges frame the lower foreground. The scene should feel stunning, elemental, immense, and visually clear.
Style/medium: Intricate engraved and etched illustration. Very thin, confident crimson contour lines trace the knife-edged mountain facets, billowing cloud rims, spiraling wind ribbons, and horizontal lake ripples. Restrained fine cross-hatching gives selected cloud and rock surfaces depth. Bold dark graphic silhouettes and beautiful intentional negative space balance the detail. A premium old engraving transformed into a severe contemporary two-color graphic.
Composition/framing: Cinematic wide alpine vista, strong central-right peak with the swirling storm arching around it, lake in the lower third, tiny pines for scale. Artwork occupies the middle and lower areas. Substantial calm dark negative space along the top and all outer edges, with lines tapering naturally into the dark background, so it sits seamlessly on a dark page. Clean readable silhouette at small size. Keep the outermost 5 percent near-black and unmarked. Do not add a frame.
Color palette: Only near-black void #0a0e13, bright Storm Red #ff3b3b, and deep red #b91c1c. Background must be near-black #0a0e13. Crimson linework, deep-red restrained cross-hatching, large dark areas. No white, gray, orange, pink, or additional colors. No glow.
Constraints: NEW original illustration, not a brand board, page screenshot, or mockup. No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, labels, badges, grids, borders, watermarks, stock 3D shapes, neon gradients, flat filled red skies, or photographic rendering. Powerful but sparse enough to feel intentional. The storm is physical cloud and wind linework, never a branded symbol.
